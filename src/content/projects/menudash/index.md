## Change a price once. Your website menu is right.

Most restaurants keep their menu in a file they edit, and the website menu is a copy of it: a PDF, a picture, or a page someone typed in by hand. Every price change means updating it twice, and the website is the copy that falls behind.

MenuDash makes your menu spreadsheet the website menu. You upload the file, and the menu page updates by itself: in German, English and Chinese, with photos and diet filters, on every guest’s phone. [See it running on afatt.ch](https://afatt.ch/menu/).

## What changes for you

- **One file for every menu.** A new price, a new dish, a dish that’s gone: change it once in Excel and upload it. The same file also goes into [menuGen](/projects/menugen/), our tool for printed menus, so the print PDF is current too.
- **Every guest finds their menu.** One QR code on the table. Each guest picks their language and filters for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, lactose-free or spicy on their own phone, instead of you keeping a separate menu for each.
- **A wrong file never breaks the page.** Every upload gets a short report. A file that isn’t a menu is refused and the live menu stays online, and the last five uploads can be put back with one click.
- **Guests order knowing what they want.** They put dishes on a selection while they browse and show it to your staff. [More below](#your-staff-still-take-the-order).

Also in MenuDash: the note on where the meat and fish come from that Swiss rules require, translated into the menu languages; a block of recommended dishes for the home page; your own colours, fonts and diet icons; and all dish photos uploaded at once, with a list of the ones still missing.

![The MenuDash page in the WordPress dashboard after an upload: a confirmation, the menu and photo upload boxes, the Download menu (CSV) button, and the check list of dishes](./dashboard-upload.webp)

## Your staff still take the order

A busy room and two languages are where orders go wrong. Most QR menus fix that by letting the guest order from the menu on their phone. MenuDash does the opposite: the phone helps the guest talk to your staff.

- **Nothing to remember.** Guests tap + on the dishes they want while they browse.
- **Easy for your staff to read.** Each dish shows its number and its German name under the guest’s language, so the guest shows it instead of pronouncing it, and both can check the order against it.
- **A question starts a conversation.** A guest can mark a dish with a question for your staff.
- **Shared as a picture.** With your address and a QR code to your menu: for the group chat before a dinner, or sent to you with a takeaway order. It carries no prices, because prices change.
- **Nothing is ordered online.** A person still takes the order. You switch it on in the dashboard.
- **Your guests’ privacy stays on their phone.** The selection is kept on the guest’s phone only, gone when they close the page, with no account, no name and no phone number. The picture is made on the phone and goes only to whoever the guest sends it to. With the Statistics add-on, your site counts how often each dish is chosen, as a daily total, never anyone’s selection.

## How it works

1. **We set it up.** You send us your current menu, dish photos, logo, address and opening hours, and a login to your WordPress site; no site yet, and we set one up. We put the menu into the spreadsheet, fill everything in and check every dish against its photo before anything goes live.
2. **You upload.** When something changes, you change the spreadsheet and upload it on the MenuDash page in your WordPress dashboard, in German, English or Chinese. A short guide shows each step with a screenshot: a new menu, new hours, a holiday.
3. **Guests scan.** You print the QR table cards straight from the dashboard, with your Wi-Fi on them if you like. Guests scan, and the menu opens on their phone.

## Add-ons

MenuDash covers the menu. Five add-ons keep the rest of what guests look up current, from the same dashboard page. Add one when you need it; Shop needs Restaurant, and the others work on their own.

### Restaurant

Address, phone, links and opening hours, entered once, with an “open now” badge. A notice for a holiday or a temporary closure appears and disappears by itself. Search engines and chat apps get the details and a share picture, with no SEO plugin needed.

![The opening hours form: time pickers per day, and a preview of how the hours read on the site](./addon-restaurant.webp)

### Specials

Today’s specials and the lunch menu of the week, in the same style and languages as the menu, each from its own short spreadsheet.

![Today’s specials on the site: starters, main courses and sides in German, English and Chinese, with diet marks and prices](./addon-specials.webp)

### Gift Cards

A gift card order form in the website’s language. Orders arrive by e-mail with your logo, and spam is kept out.

![The gift card page: the card picture beside a form with amounts, pickup or post, name, e-mail and an order button](./addon-giftcards.webp)

### Shop

A page for things guests take home: a jar of the house chili oil, cookies, or chairs you no longer need. Each item has photos, a price, how many are left and whether it is available, reserved or sold. Guests call or write and pick up at the restaurant; there is no cart and no online payment. Shop works together with the Restaurant add-on.

![The shop page with sample items: category buttons, and a jar of chili oil, a bag of cookies and a set of chairs marked Reserved, each with its price and how many are left](./addon-shop.webp)

### Statistics

Which dishes guests stop at, which filters and languages they use, which dishes they choose for their selection, where menu visits come from, including from a shared selection, and what they do next: call, find the way, order, book. Mark the day something changed, such as new photos or a new menu, and see the weeks after it next to the weeks before. No cookies.

![Before and after a change: visits, dishes seen, photos opened, filters, speed, where visits come from, calls, directions, orders and bookings, 14 days before and after a marked day](./addon-statistics.webp)

## Good to know

- **You are not locked in.** MenuDash and the MenuDash Theme are open source, on GitHub for anyone to see ([MenuDash](https://github.com/yingshiuan/menudash), [MenuDash Theme](https://github.com/yingshiuan/menudash-theme)). If you stop working with us, your menu keeps running. What you pay us for is the setup, the add-ons, and someone who answers when something needs to change.
- **Search engines read every dish.** The menu is real text, not a PDF or a picture, and each dish is marked up with its names in three languages, its price and its diet marks.
- **Statistics counts without watching anyone.** No cookies, nothing stored in the guest’s browser and no IP address saved: only totals per dish, button and day, kept for at most 25 months. A paragraph for your privacy policy comes with it.
- **It runs on WordPress.** A site on Wix or Squarespace does not fit, and we will say so.
- **German, English and Chinese are built in.** Other languages are possible, but they are extra work.
- **One menu per site.**
- **It is the menu guests read.** Recipes, food cost and stock belong to kitchen software, and MenuDash does not do them.
