## The short version

If you reject analytics, or simply never answer the question, this site writes
nothing to your device except the record of that decision. We do not sell
anything, do not run ads, and do not build a profile of you. What follows is the
long version, written to be checked rather than skimmed.

## Who is responsible

insdash, Zürich, Switzerland — <insdash.io@gmail.com>.

We are a two-person studio, and the same two people who built this site answer
anything you write to us about it. We have not appointed a data protection
officer; at this size, holding this little, neither Swiss nor EU law requires
one.

## What happens when you just read a page

Two things, both before you have chosen anything.

**Hosting.** The site is static — pages are built in advance and served by
GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). Like any web server, GitHub's
receives and logs the request: IP address, time, page, browser, referring page.
That happens before any of our code runs, we cannot switch it off, and GitHub
does not pass those logs on to us.

**Fonts and icons.** Every page loads typefaces from Google Fonts
(`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com`) and icon styles from Cloudflare's
cdnjs (`cdnjs.cloudflare.com`). Your browser requests those files directly, so
your IP address reaches Google and Cloudflare on every page view, whatever you
answered about analytics. This one is on us rather than on you: serving the
files from our own domain would remove both third parties, and it is on the
list.

## Analytics, and the choice you were given

We use Google Analytics 4 (property `{measurementId}`) to see which work people
actually read. It is off until you allow it.

Before the tag loads, this site sets Google's Consent Mode to deny storage. So
unless you press Accept:

- no analytics cookies are written to your device
- your IP address is truncated before storage (`anonymize_ip`)
- advertising storage and personalisation stay denied — those stay denied
  permanently, whatever you choose, because we do not run ads or build
  audiences

If you accept, Google Analytics writes the cookies listed below and we get
aggregate numbers: pages opened, approximate location by country and city,
device type, and where visitors arrived from. We never see who you are. We do
not export it and do not combine it with anything else.

If you reject, or never answer, the tag stays in denied mode — but it does still
load, and Google receives one cookieless request from your browser carrying your
IP address and the page you are on. That is how the tag reports "no consent
here". It is worth saying plainly: rejecting means no cookies and no persistent
identity, not zero contact with Google.

## What is stored on your device

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics, only after you accept | Distinguishes one browser from another | 2 years |
| `_ga_{measurementId}` | Google Analytics, only after you accept | Keeps session state for the property | 2 years |
| `analytics_consent` | This site | Remembers whether you accepted or rejected | 6 months |

`analytics_consent` is browser storage rather than a cookie, which means it is
never attached to a request and never leaves your device — not to us, not to
anyone. It holds one word and a date. It expires after six months, at which
point the bar asks again, because a decision made once should not stand for
ever.

Older Google tags also used `_gid` and `_gat`. We do not set them, but if your
browser still carries one from a previous visit, rejecting clears those too.

## Changing your mind

**Cookie settings**, in the footer of every page, reopens the consent bar. Press
Reject there and we switch analytics storage back to denied immediately and
delete the `_ga` cookies already on your device. It is one click — the same one
click that accepting was. Withdrawing consent must not be harder than giving it,
and here it is not.

## If you email us

The address on this site is a plain `mailto:` link. There is no form and nothing
is submitted through the site, so an email to us is an ordinary email: it
reaches a Google-hosted mailbox, and Google processes it as our mail provider.

We keep project correspondence for as long as the engagement runs, and afterwards
for as long as Swiss bookkeeping rules require. Enquiries that do not turn into
work are deleted once they are no longer of any use.

## Embedded content on project pages

Two case studies embed material hosted elsewhere — a Figma prototype on the A
Fatt study, and a GitHub badge image from shields.io on menuGen. Opening those
pages contacts those providers directly, and they may set their own cookies
under their own policies.

They are page content rather than analytics, so they sit outside the consent
bar. If you would rather not load them, do not open those pages.

## Where the data goes

Hosting, analytics, fonts, icons and mail are all United States providers.
Switzerland recognises transfers to US companies certified under the Swiss–US
Data Privacy Framework, and the EU–US framework covers visitors from the EEA on
the same basis. Google, GitHub (Microsoft) and Cloudflare are certified under
those frameworks.

## Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and under the GDPR if you are in
the EU or EEA, you can ask for access to what we hold about you, correction,
deletion, restriction, a portable copy, and you can object to processing.

In practice we hold almost nothing that identifies anyone, so most requests will
get the answer "we have nothing on you" — which we will say plainly rather than
sending you a form. Write to <insdash.io@gmail.com> and we will answer within 30
days.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Federal Data Protection and
Information Commissioner in Switzerland, or to the supervisory authority where
you live in the EEA.

## Changes to this policy

The date at the top is the one that matters. If we ever add something that
collects more than what is described here, we will ask for consent again rather
than quietly widening what you already agreed to.
